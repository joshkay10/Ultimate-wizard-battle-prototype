# Wizard Battle — Rules

An **Into the Breach** holdout. **9×9**. You defend a city of crystals from vek. There is no Vs mode.

Four wizards start **off the field**. A **summon spell** brings them on. Each body on the board gets **one move** and **one attack** per turn.

## How a match starts

Play opens on **The Pass** (island 1). Clear an island to unlock the next. **Retry** restarts that same island.

Both sides of the campaign use the Team page loadout, padded to **four**. Named islands still pin their scripted kits first. All four wait in hand until you summon them.

**Three crystals** a camp (2 HP each), packed in one or two clusters. There are no enemy crystals. The arena is a **named island** — the pass, the canal, the alley, the moat — not a random scatter of rocks.

**One vek** is already on the board, with **no telegraph and no incoming marks** on round 1.

## Turn

**Every onboard wizard may move once and attack once**, then the vek execute.

1. **Summon** — click an off-field wizard, then an open tile. **Costs 1 mana.** They land immediately and **can act this turn**. You may summon more than one if you can pay. You cannot summon onto a nexus, mountain, water, void, occupied tile, or incoming pawn mark.
2. **Move** — each onboard wizard that has not moved may walk once. Other wizards, living crystals, mountains, water, and voids block the path. You cannot walk onto water or a void. **Undo** the last move of a wizard if they have not attacked yet.
3. **Attack** — each onboard wizard that has not attacked may strike once: **stock**, or (once unlocked) spell 1 or spell 2.
4. **End turn** — vek strike, then walk and telegraph. Your turn auto-ends when nobody has a summon, move, or attack left. A pending undo still counts as something to do.

## Mana

One pool. Round 1 starts at **2**. Each later round the max goes up by 1, cap **6**, and the pool refills to max.

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Stock | **0** |
| Summon | **1** |
| Spell 1 | **1×** that kit’s mana weight |
| Spell 2 | **2×** that kit’s mana weight |

## Stock and growth

Every wizard has the same **stock** punch: **1 damage, 1 push, range 1** (adjacent, cardinals). It is always free.

Better spells are locked until that body **gets better**:

- **0 kills** — stock only
- **1 kill** — spell 1 unlocks
- **2 kills** — spell 2 unlocks

A dunk (push into water or a void) counts. Kills are per wizard, this match.

## Team

**Pyre, Rime, and Squall are live.** Cairn, Volt, and Chrono are on the bench. Bring exactly four from the live three (copies allowed). Named islands lock their scripted kits and pad to four. The Team page has a randomize button. The loadout is saved on this device.

Default team: Pyre (Stream + Lance), Rime (Sheet + Pulse), Squall (Gust + Draft), Pyre (Cinder + Inferno).

## Enemies

Dark discs, white icons, a facing wedge for aim. Each body is one Into the Breach archetype, 1 damage. They attack **wizards and the city**. Red tiles show the strike they will take after your turn — only if that strike hits someone. Shoving a body into water or a void is a **dunk**.

| Body | HP | Move | Trick |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mite | 1 | 4 | Small, fast melee fodder. **One hit and it pops.** Two can **stack on one tile** to hit for **2** — but the doubled tile is still 1 HP |
| Brute | 3 | 3 | Melee the adjacent tile (Scorpion) |
| Charger | 4 | 3 | Charge up to 3 tiles. 1 dmg, then **push 1** if they live. Dies in water (Beetle) |
| Bomber | 3 | 2 | Line 4, 1 dmg, first wizard or nexus (Firefly) |
| Golem | 7–8 | 2 | Melee the adjacent tile, **push 1**. Too tough to trade blows with — **shove it into water or a void** |

They beeline the nearest nexus. If they cannot hit the city this turn they punch a wizard in range, otherwise they walk toward the city. They never telegraph empty air. They will not aim a charge into water or a void.

A match has a **spawn budget** of invaders (opening counts) — **4 / 6 / 8 / 10** on missions 1–4, **10** on a free island. At most **3** on the board at once (mission 1 caps at 2). New bodies stream in **one hole per enemy phase**. On a named mission the remaining budget still arrives after a wipe. Incoming holes open on **open field ground** — not the last two rows, not city, water, or mountains.

Strikes resolve one body at a time, **north to south, then west to east** (the telegraph shows 1, 2, 3). Then each body walks in that same order and declares its next strike.

## Unique casts

- **Stream / gust / sheet / draft / brand / lock / tug** — straight line, cardinals only. Stops on the first wizard or crystal. Mountains block. Water and voids do not. Gust still fans fire.
- **Lance** — a piercing beam. It does **not** stop on the first body: it hits every wizard along the line of 4 and paints fire the whole way. Mountains block it; it flies over crystals without chipping them.
- **Pulse / jolt** — hitting any highlighted neighbor fires the whole ring. Empty tiles, allies, enemies, and crystals in the ring all count. Pulse survivors are pushed away from the caster.
- **Burst (inferno, blizzard, gale, quake, arc, cinder, spike)** — click an aim tile in range. The square around it is the effect. Friendly fire is on. Blizzard freeze is ice paint, not a status.
- **Raise** — an empty tile in range becomes a temporary mountain. Blocks walk, melee, cast, and push until it crumbles.
- **Bolt** — line of 4. Silences the wizard it hits. Jumps along water. Grounds on a raised mountain (no jump).
- **Swap / loop** — trade places with a wizard, or blink to an empty tile. **Step** is blink only. Landing on water or a void still kills you.

## Combat

- **Stock** hits an adjacent tile (up, down, left, right). Mountains, water, and voids are not stock targets.
- A hit deals that attack’s damage, then **pushes** the target that many tiles away (0 push means they stay).
- **Crash:** if the push hits a wall (edge, mountain, or living crystal), leftover knockback **smashes**. Crash damage on a wizard is the leftover pips (at least 1). Hitting **another wizard** smashes both, then leftover knock transfers. A crash into a **crystal** still deals **1**. Water and voids are not walls — you are pushed onto the tile and you fall.
- A hit that deals more than remaining HP still **pushes the body**. Overflow pops as overkill.
- Friendly fire is on.
- HP 0 = dead. That body does not come back.
- Paint spells leave an elemental trail. Hitting an **empty tile is valid**.
  - **Fire:** walking, sliding, or arriving onto it costs 1 damage.
  - **Ice:** a push that enters ice does not spend a knockback pip, so you keep sliding.
  - **Wind:** if you finish a move or push on wind, it carries you one more tile in that direction. A gust that hits fire **fans** it.

## Terrain

- **Mountains** are impassable: no walk, no stock, no cast through. A push into a mountain is blocked (and crashes).
- **Water** kills on enter. Line spells fly over it.
- **Voids** open when a crystal drops to 0 HP. Entering a void kills you, same as water.

## Winning

Checked after each side’s turn (not on that side’s very first turn, except a wipe, which also checks as soon as the last pawn dies):

- You **win** if no enemy pawns are on the board or incoming. **Named missions** also wait until the spawn budget is spent — wiping the opener does not skip the rest of the wave.
- You **lose** if **all** of your clustered nexuses are at 0 HP, or you have no wizards left in hand or on the board.

## Balance

These numbers are the live contract.

- **Four off the field** so round 1 is a deploy, not a brawl.
- **Summon costs 1** so round 1 (2 mana) can land two bodies, or one body and a later spell.
- **Stock 1/1/1** so every kit shares a punch. Identity lives in the unlocked spells.
- **1 move + 1 attack each** so you plan the whole squad, then the vek resolve — Into the Breach pace, not chess.
- **Kills unlock spells** so the Pass teaches punch first, Gust second.
- **Mana 2 → 6** so spell 2 is the grow-in.
- **Crystals 2 HP, crash 1** so the city is a clock, not a sponge.
- Hunter vek still prefer the city. Cap 3 on the board.

If a kit feels like a delete button, cut damage or range — do not put everyone back on the field at the opening.
